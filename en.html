My Vision

I am Carlos López. Audiovisual communicator since 2022 and wedding photographer in Cartagena with a decade of experience dedicated to understanding the heartbeat of every celebration.

My approach shies away from the conventional, not just to break the mold, but because I believe the best stories deserve to be told with truth, spontaneity, and an overwhelming joy that infects everyone in front of the camera.

Every couple holds a unique universe. My job is to decipher it, transform the light of our city into art, and deliver a visual legacy that, twenty years from now, will bring back exactly the same emotion.
